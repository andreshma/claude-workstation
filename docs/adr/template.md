# NNNN. Short title of the decision

- Status: Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by [NNNN](nnnn-....md)
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD

## Context and problem statement

What situation forces a decision, and what question it has to answer. Two or three short paragraphs. State facts and
link the evidence (an issue, a measurement, a review).

## Decision drivers

- The forces that matter for this decision: a requirement, a constraint, a risk, a cost.

## Considered options

- **A. First option.** One or two sentences, with its main advantage and its main cost.
- **B. Second option.** The same.

## Decision

The option chosen and how it is applied: what changes, where, and what stays as it was.

## Consequences

- Positive: what gets better.
- Negative: what gets worse, what is given up and what has to be maintained from now on.
